# Data provenance and terms

Everything in [`data/`](data/) is a **derived summary statistic** computed by this project —
scores, ranks, percentile transforms, per-tissue medians, and single-cell detection fractions.
No raw sequence, individual-level, or controlled-access data is redistributed in this repository.
To obtain the primary data, go to the sources below.

| Source | Used for | Access | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SURFY** surfaceome | Candidate pool of 2,886 cell-surface proteins, with Almén class and surface-confidence labels | [wlab.ethz.ch/surfaceome](https://wlab.ethz.ch/surfaceome/) | Published resource; cite Bausch-Fluck et al. 2018 |
| **TCGA-BRCA PanCancer Atlas** (via cBioPortal) | Tumor expression, PAM50 Basal-like n=171 as a TNBC proxy | [cbioportal.org](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=brca_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018) | All TCGA publication moratoria are lifted; TCGA asks to be acknowledged (see below) |
| **GTEx** | Normal-tissue expression for the safety and tissue-orthogonality axes | [gtexportal.org](https://gtexportal.org/home/downloads/) | Open-access summary-level data |
| **CZ CELLxGENE Discover Census** (2025-11-08) | TNBC single-cell atlas — 161,048 cells, 48,862 malignant — for intratumoral coverage and co-expression | [cellxgene.cziscience.com](https://cellxgene.cziscience.com/) | CC BY 4.0, attribution to the original data contributors |
| **UCSC Xena TOIL** (`TcgaTargetGtex_rsem_gene_tpm`) | GTEx and TCGA reprocessed on one TPM scale, for the comparable organ heatmap | [xenabrowser.net](https://xenabrowser.net/datapages/) | Public |

## Acknowledgements to include when reusing

**TCGA.** The results here are in whole or part based upon data generated by the TCGA Research
Network: <https://www.cancer.gov/tcga>.

**CZ CELLxGENE Discover.** Single-cell data were obtained from CZ CELLxGENE Discover Census;
individual source studies retain their own citation requirements.

**GTEx.** The Genotype-Tissue Expression (GTEx) Project was supported by the Common Fund of the
Office of the Director of the National Institutes of Health, and by NCI, NHGRI, NHLBI, NIDA,
NIMH, and NINDS.

## What is *not* here

- Raw counts, BAM files, or any individual-level genotype or expression data.
- The intermediate matrices the numbered scripts consume. The scripts in [`code/`](code/) read
  from `inputs/<filename>`; those inputs are rebuilt from the sources in the table above.
  See [`code/README.md`](code/README.md).
